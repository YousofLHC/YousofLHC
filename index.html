01 · about

A mind that treats science and signal as one language.

I am an AI researcher and engineer with a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, focused on the intersection of probabilistic inference, graph-structured data, and computational science. My work spans Approximate Message Passing (AMP), Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Kalman filtering, and generative models applied to drug design, metabolic engineering, material informatics, and autonomous AI agents.

I believe the most impactful machine learning is built on a strong foundation of statistics, optimization, and domain science. My research style combines rigorous theory with shipping practical systems — from high-dimensional sparse recovery to end-to-end molecular property pipelines and literature-mining agent workflows.

I am actively looking for PhD positions, research collaborations, and consulting engagements where I can push the boundaries of learning on scientific data.

→ Currently: diffusion-based molecular generation + AMP-inspired graph architectures, and agentic literature systems for metabolic engineering.

GAMP-GNN

Message Passing for Model-Based Compressed Sensing

Guided Diffusion over Graphs for De Novo Drug Design

An equivariant diffusion model generating valid, synthesizable molecules under target-aware guidance, with RDKit-based property filters and a fragment-level latent space.
