## 1 · The linear-Gaussian state-space model

The filter assumes a process evolving linearly with Gaussian noise:

$$
x_{t} = A x_{t-1} + w_t, \qquad w_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, Q)
$$

with observations

$$
z_t = H x_t + v_t, \qquad v_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R).
$$

The goal: maintain the filtering distribution $p(x_t \mid z_{1:t})$, which turns out
to stay Gaussian forever, so it's fully described by a mean and covariance.

## 2 · Prediction (prior) step

Given $p(x_{t-1} \mid z_{1:t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{t-1}, P_{t-1})$:

$$
\hat{x}_{t|t-1} = A \hat{x}_{t-1}
$$

$$
P_{t|t-1} = A P_{t-1} A^\top + Q
$$

*Proof sketch:* mean of an affine transform, plus the added process noise covariance.

## 3 · Update (correction) step

The innovation carries the new information:

$$
\tilde{y}_t = z_t - H \hat{x}_{t|t-1}
$$

The Kalman gain weighs prior vs measurement by their relative covariance:

$$
K_t = P_{t|t-1} H^\top \big(H P_{t|t-1} H^\top + R\big)^{-1}
$$

Posterior mean and covariance:

$$
\hat{x}_t = \hat{x}_{t|t-1} + K_t \tilde{y}_t,
\qquad
P_t = (I - K_t H) P_{t|t-1}
$$

<Callout type="note" title="Derive the gain yourself">
Minimize $\mathbb{E}\|x_t - \hat{x}_t\|^2$. Differentiating w.r.t. $K_t$ and setting
to zero gives exactly the formula above — the estimator is simultaneously the
MMSE, MAP, and conditional mean because everything is Gaussian.
</Callout>

## 4 · The information form (why it matters for message passing)

Inverting the update gives the *information form* of the filter — this is the form
that makes Kalman filtering look identical to belief propagation:

$$
P_t^{-1} = P_{t|t-1}^{-1} + H^\top R^{-1} H,
\qquad
P_t^{-1} \hat{x}_t = P_{t|t-1}^{-1} \hat{x}_{t|t-1} + H^\top R^{-1} z_t.
$$

These are exactly the sum-product rules from the *factor graphs* article: precision
adds, and the information vector accumulates measurements. **A Kalman filter is
Gaussian belief propagation on a chain.**

## 5 · Connection map

| Filter | Message-passing concept |
| --- | --- |
| Prior covariance $P_{t\|t-1}$ | uncertainty of the forward message |
| Gain $K_t$ | Bayesian weighting of evidence |
| Information form | factor-to-variable message |
| Smoothing pass | backward messages |

## Practice problems

1. Derive $K_t$ by differentiating the MMSE cost.
2. Show that for $R \to \infty$, $\hat{x}_t \to$ pure prediction.
3. Prove that for scalar systems, $P_t$ converges to the unique solution of the
   Riccati equation $P = A P A^\top + Q - A P H^\top (H P H^\top + R)^{-1} H P A^\top$.
